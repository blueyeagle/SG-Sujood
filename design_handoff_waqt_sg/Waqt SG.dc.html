Waqt SG · iOS prototype

Prayer times and prayer spaces, for moving around Singapore

Timings follow the MUIS daily schedule. The home screen pairs the countdown with the nearest usable space, because the recurring question on a workday is not "when" but "where, and can I make it".

Assumptions
  • Timings sourced from MUIS; no calculation-method picker.
  • Reminders are silent by default — vibration plus a 15-minute nudge. No adhan audio.
  • A space is described by two facts only: where it is in the building, and how long it takes to walk there.
  • Mall musollah data is community-maintained, so every listing shows when it was last confirmed.
Screens

The frame is live — the tab bar, cards and toggles all work.
